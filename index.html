Unshort Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how the Unshort Android app ("Unshort", "the app", "we") handles information. Unshort is developed by Amrish Sharma. It helps you notice and cut down on short-form video scrolling (such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok) by either blocking those feeds or tracking how much you swipe and how long you watch.

The short version

  • Everything stays on your phone. Unshort has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
  • We don't collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • The accessibility service reads what is on screen only in supported apps, only to detect short-form video feeds, and nothing it reads is saved except swipe counts and time totals.

1. Accessibility service

Unshort's core features rely on Android's AccessibilityService API. When you turn on the Unshort accessibility service, the app can see the content of the screen in these apps only: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X (Twitter), Chrome and Snapchat.

It uses this access to:

  • Detect short-form video feeds (for example, recognising that the YouTube Shorts player or Instagram Reels viewer is on screen).
  • Block them in Block mode, by going back or stopping the scroll.
  • Count swipes and time in Track mode, and show them in two small bubbles on top of the feed.

Screen content is examined in memory at the moment it is shown and then discarded. Unshort does not record, store or transmit what you watch, the text on screen, video titles, usernames, messages or anything you type. The only results kept are the counts and durations described in section 3.

You can turn the service off at any time in Settings → Accessibility on your device. Blocking and tracking stop immediately.

3. Information stored on your device

To make its features work, Unshort saves the following in its private app storage on your phone:

  • Daily swipe counts and time spent in Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok, kept for the last 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Daily usage time for apps you have put in a limited category, used to enforce your time limits.
  • Your settings: Block or Track mode, app categories, time limits and whether a Focus Session is active.

This data never leaves your device through Unshort, and other apps cannot read it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Device backups

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include Unshort's settings and statistics in your device backup (for example, your Google account backup) so they can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and your backup provider under their own privacy terms; Unshort does not send or receive it itself. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information or send any data to servers. The app has no internet access.
  • We don't sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • We don't show ads or use analytics, crash reporting or other third-party SDKs.
  • We don't require an account or sign-in.

5. Your choices

  • Stop tracking or blocking: turn off the Unshort accessibility service in your device's Accessibility settings.
  • Delete your data: go to Settings → Apps → Unshort → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Switch modes: choose Block or Track in the app's Settings.

6. Children

Unshort is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Security

All data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server copy of your data that could be exposed.

8. Changes to this policy

If Unshort's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
